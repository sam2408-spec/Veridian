How it works

  1. You apply here with your qualifications, a certificate and two references.
  2. The Veridian Academy team checks every application and contacts your referees. Nobody teaches until they are approved.
  3. Approved teachers run group classes only, by free video call (Jitsi Meet), at times posted on the school page.
  4. Every class has a second adult (a moderator) present. Children join with a first name only.

Our safeguarding rules

  • No one-to-one or private contact with any child — not by video, phone, WhatsApp, social media or email.
  • Never ask for a child's surname, address, phone number, photo or location.
  • No recording or screenshots of children.
  • Do not ask children to describe what they have been through. If a child tells you they are in danger or being hurt, stay calm, listen, and report it to the moderator and the Veridian team the same day.
  • Teach kindly: no shouting, no political or religious pressure, no promises you cannot keep.
  • Any breach means you will be removed straight away.

Application

Two references

People who know your work with children — not family members.
